## Peer policy and result

Before selection, the policy admitted listed semiconductor operating companies with positive annual GAAP diluted EPS, material data-center compute/AI-accelerator business, and comparable economics. AMD qualified only with a material mismatch because Client and Gaming revenue remains significant; Broadcom was excluded because infrastructure software and diversified operations fail the policy. The resulting AMD historical P/E was 190.04x and implied $931.18 for NVIDIA using annual GAAP EPS. It is an equity-value multiple and a **qualified one-peer reference**, not an input to the DCF range.

No final EV multiple or transaction value is used as a decision input: a comparable control/synergy context and a sufficiently comparable EV-multiple peer set were not established. That limitation is disclosed rather than filled with an invented number.
