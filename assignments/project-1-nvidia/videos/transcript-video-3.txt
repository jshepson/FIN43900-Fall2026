Video 3 - Committee Defense

My recommendation is watch/defer NVIDIA and do not initiate at the September 24, 2026 market reference of 221.70 dollars per share. My FCFF valuation range is 137.31 to 280.88 dollars per share, with a base case of 193.14 dollars.

The market reference is above my base value. The high case exceeds the market price, but it requires a stronger growth path, higher margins, lower WACC, and higher terminal growth. I do not treat that conditional outcome as enough evidence to initiate a position.

The main operating driver is durability of Data Center demand. The low case combines slower revenue growth and lower margin with a higher discount rate and lower terminal growth. The model still articulates, but value falls to 137.31 dollars per share.

The largest limitation is that terminal value is 77 percent of base enterprise value. I also treat the AMD historical P/E as a qualified reference rather than averaging it into the DCF because the peer has different revenue mix and market expectations.

I would reconsider initiation if two reported quarters support the modeled demand, margin, and cash-conversion path and the share price offers a margin of safety to my base value. I would move to do-not-initiate if growth, margins, cash conversion, or the enterprise-to-equity bridge weaken materially. My committee ask is to keep NVIDIA on watch/defer and refresh the model after the next filing.
